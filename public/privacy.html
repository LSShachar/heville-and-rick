Privacy

For hevilleandrick.com and theorangery.hevilleandrick.com. Last updated 14 October 2026.

What these sites keep

  • The bag. When something is added to the bag, the browser keeps the identifier of a Shopify cart so the bag is still there on return. Nothing else is stored in the browser.
  • Payment. Checkout, payment, shipping details and receipts are handled by Shopify on its own pages. Card details never reach these sites.
  • Visit counts. Cloudflare Web Analytics counts page visits without cookies and without following anyone across sites.
  • No accounts and no advertising trackers. Nothing is sold or shared for advertising.

Services these sites rely on

  • Cloudflare serves the pages. Shopify supplies the products and the bag. Google Fonts supplies some lettering. Each receives the ordinary technical details of a web request, such as a network address.

Who runs these sites

House of Heville & Rick, a house of Ontokinesis Applied Systems LLC, a Delaware series limited liability company. Questions about privacy may be sent through the contact page of ontokinesis.com.
